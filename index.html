OpenChat is a privacy-first AI chat app for iPhone and iPad. Bring your own API key, talk to any model you like, and keep every conversation on your own device.

Why OpenChat

🔑

Bring your own key

Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, GLM, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint with your own API key. You pay providers directly for what you use — no markup, no middleman.

🧭

Hermes Agent, locally

Use OpenChat Pro to connect Hermes on your Mac as a local OpenAI-compatible backend. Copy the generated start command, run it in Terminal, and keep your tools, memory, and skills on your own machine.

🔁

Every model, one place

Switch models mid-conversation. Compare answers from different providers side by side and pick the one that works best for the task at hand.

🛡️

Private by design

Conversations are stored locally on your device. OpenChat has no servers and collects no analytics. Requests go straight from your phone to your chosen provider.

🎙️

Speak, don't type

Dictate messages by voice with on-device speech recognition, and keep the conversation flowing hands-free when you want to.

☁️

Your backup, your cloud

Optional iCloud backup keeps a copy of your conversations in your own private iCloud — not on ours, because we don't have one.

⚡

Fast and focused

Streaming responses, Markdown rendering, conversation search, pinned threads, and a native interface built with SwiftUI that feels right at home on iOS.

How it works

  1. Download OpenChat from the App Store.
  2. Add a provider — paste an API key from any supported service.
  3. Chat with the model of your choice, switch anytime.

OpenChat Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks Hermes Agent, unlimited providers, and premium conveniences across your devices. The core chat experience works with your first provider for free.
